## Data Collection

This site does not collect personal data. No forms, no accounts, no analytics.

Hosting infrastructure may retain standard server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) for operational purposes. These logs are not linked to any identity and are not shared.

## Browser Storage

This site stores data in your browser only to preserve your preferences across page loads. No data is sent to any server, and nothing is used for tracking or profiling.

**Necessary**: a single browser cookie records your consent decision. A small number of localStorage entries preserve appearance and accessibility settings without consent, as they are required for the interface to render correctly.

**Functional**: if you accept the functional category, your accessibility preferences and any fetched repository metadata are cached in localStorage to avoid repeated network requests.

**Session only**: sidebar scroll position and collapsed state are kept in sessionStorage and discarded when the tab is closed.

## Third-Party Services

This site does not load Google Analytics or any other third-party tracking service.
